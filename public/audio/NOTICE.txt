Haru Japanese — 14 pronunciation samples (あ-row, か-row, basic greetings)

These are synthesized Japanese speech samples, not recordings of a native speaker.
Generated with Open JTalk via pyopenjtalk-plus 0.4.1.post10, using HTS Voice Mei
(mei_normal.htsvoice), at speed 0.8. Peak-normalized and encoded to MP3 (96 kbps).
